# Phase 2 Scope Summary for Independent Review

**Sanitization:** This document contains only a high-level summary of Phase 2 scope and acceptance areas. It contains no application source, credentials, personal information, live records, or production data.

**Authority and limits:** Prepared from the project scope notes available to the owner. It is not a verbatim copy of the private project documents and does not replace the approved Phase 2 scope gate. The owner must verify this summary against the authoritative scope before treating it as final.

## Objective

Phase 2 is intended to establish readiness for a controlled field pilot and recovery rehearsal. It is a gated, limited evaluation, not a public production launch or broad rollout.

## In-scope readiness outcomes

The acceptance areas recorded for Phase 2 are:

1. A repeatable pilot with defined workflows and observable expected outcomes.
2. A recovery drill with reproducible steps and verification.
3. Review of roles and role-specific responsibilities.
4. A reproducible operating runbook.
5. Evidence and risk review sufficient to support a reasoned readiness decision.
6. Explicit owner acceptance against the agreed gate before Phase 2 is declared complete.

Reviewers should assess whether these outcomes are clearly specified, objectively testable, and supported by appropriate evidence. This public packet does not itself contain the evidence needed to mark them complete.

## Exclusions

This review does not authorize or request:

- access to the production website, server, APIs, databases, or accounts;
- penetration testing, scanning, or attempts to bypass access controls;
- handling credentials, personal information, or actual farm records;
- changes to production, deployment, release, or broad rollout;
- treating community feedback as an approval or completion decision.

## Readiness decision rule

A Phase 2 completion decision requires the owner to compare the authoritative approved scope with pilot and recovery evidence, review role/runbook readiness and risks, resolve or explicitly accept blocking findings, and record the final acceptance. Public reviewers provide recommendations only.

## Reviewer task

Review the scope and readiness areas for ambiguity, missing acceptance criteria, unclear ownership, inadequate evidence expectations, and unsafe assumptions. Use the [reviewer worksheet](REVIEWER_WORKSHEET.md) and submit actionable items through GitHub Issues. Keep observations limited to this public packet.
