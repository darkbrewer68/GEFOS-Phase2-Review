## Finding format

For each issue, report one finding with:

- **Location:** document and section in this public packet.
- **Observation:** what the packet actually says or omits.
- **Impact:** why it matters to a controlled pilot or recovery decision.
- **Recommendation:** a specific change to clarify the criteria or evidence needed.
- **Confidence:** high / medium / low, with assumptions stated.

Do not include personal information, credentials, real operational records, exploit details, or links to sensitive production resources. Do not present a review recommendation as a security certification or authorization to deploy.

## Important limitation

The rows begin as **Not assessed** because this public packet summarizes acceptance areas but does not include pilot records, recovery artifacts, detailed runbooks, or private implementation evidence. Reviewers must not infer that a criterion has passed merely because it appears in the list.
